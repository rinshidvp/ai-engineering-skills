# Question Bank

Checklist for Grill. Skip obviously irrelevant, err toward asking.

- **Users & access:** who sees? who doesn't? unauthorized? user types?
- **Content & data:** shown? source? missing/empty/stale? min/max? truncation?
- **Interactions:** actions? result per action? confirm/feedback? undo?
- **States:** loading, empty, error, partial, success, disabled — each looks like?
- **Validation:** rules? when checked? exact error wording?
- **Sequences:** before/after? back allowed? what preserved?
- **Devices:** mobile vs desktop? touch? breakpoints?
- **Edge:** double submit? back button? refresh mid-flow? timeout? slow net?

Pacing: 8-15 normal. Past 20 → too deep one area, step back.
Short answers = certain, fine. Long story = buried requirement — pull out: "Hearing X — hard requirement or nice-to-have?"
